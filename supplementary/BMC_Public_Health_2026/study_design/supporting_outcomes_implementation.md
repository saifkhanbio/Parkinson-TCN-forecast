# Mortality and disability supporting analyses

Specified on 30 September 2026 after prevalence/incidence, demographic, calibration and release-comparison results were inspected. This implements the supporting mortality/disability item and the YLD/YLL/DALY comparisons in locked design v1.0. It does not change the primary objective, model grids, age scope or earlier results. No new mortality/disability forecast results were used to choose the conventions below.

## Direct age-specific outcome forecasts

Evaluate deaths, years lived with disability (YLDs), years of life lost (YLLs) and disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) in all six GCC countries. Saudi Arabia remains the principal target; Jordan remains a donor. Use both sexes, all eleven ages 45–49 through 95+, the common 1990–2023 period, eight input years and horizons 1–5. Retain the 2018-origin/2023 endpoint separately from the five overlapping origins 2014–2018. Death rates are events per 100,000; disability rates are burden-years per 100,000. These are modeled GBD estimates, not independent patients or observed Poisson counts.

Each of 24 cases uses the fourteen original families plus the local and non-neural champion views, identical candidate grids, historical selection rules, five TCN seeds and limited adaptation. Choices are specific to the actual target, sex and outcome and use only completed historical five-year windows. Restrict the actual outcome and 1990-through-origin years before assigning the internal `prevalence` channel label needed by the immutable model functions; restore the true outcome in public artifacts. Prevalence-implied origin populations provide the same sensitivity weights for all outcomes and are never predictors.

Keep seven to eleven complete historical log-error blocks, centered coordinate by coordinate, with the same linear quantiles, nominal 50%/80% intervals and supplementary sparse-tail 95% intervals. Report equal-age absolute log error, rate MAE, age-specific and explicit 80+ performance, coverage, width, directional misses and 50/80 WIS on rate and log-rate scales. All families remain reported. No new interval adjustment or selection on the inspected final years is allowed. Favorable supporting comparisons cannot replace the failed joint prevalence primary criterion.

## Component-derived DALYs

Verify the source identities `DALY = YLD + YLL` on both rate and Number scales within country, sex, age and year, allowing numerical rounding only. Forecast-derived DALYs sum independently forecast YLD and YLL rates at each matching coordinate, never their logs. For the fourteen underlying families, match the same family across components. For a champion view, each component uses its own outcome-specific, sex-specific champion selected at that outer origin; those families may differ. Retain source-family provenance.

For intervals, join the YLD and YLL rate draws by country, outer origin, family/role, sex, age, horizon and **historical residual origin**, require identical complete block sets, and add the two rate draws. Calculate quantiles of the sums on the rate scale and of their logarithms on the log scale. Do not sum marginal interval endpoints or medians. The component-derived point remains the sum of component point forecasts. Compare against direct DALY forecasts from the same family/role, with no post-result substitution. Coherent accounting alone does not establish improved forecast accuracy or independent biological validity.

## Prevalence-based YLD baseline

Use the three frozen prevalence views: adapted TCN, local champion and non-neural champion. For each target, sex, age and issuance origin `u`, estimate a fixed training ratio as `sum(YLD rate, u−7..u) / sum(prevalence rate, u−7..u)`, using eight complete pre-origin years. Multiply each saved prevalence-rate point forecast by this ratio at every horizon. The ratio is an accounting baseline, not a clinical severity, progression or survival measure. The eight-year window is fixed and receives no tuning.

Construct this baseline's own prequential YLD errors at origins 2003–2013: use the prevalence forecast issued at each historical origin and the ratio computed at that same origin, then compare with the subsequently verified YLD value. At an outer origin, a prevalence champion's current sex-specific family is held fixed across its historical bank, retaining that family's originally selected historical settings, exactly as in the original champion interval procedure. No undefined early champion is invented and no family is selected using YLD evaluation errors. Baseline local/non-neural source families therefore need not match the separately fitted direct-YLD champions.

Center the resulting complete YLD log-error blocks and apply the original interval procedure. Do not multiply current prevalence residual draws by a fixed ratio and claim this represents ratio forecast uncertainty. Report baseline-versus-direct YLD errors and uncertainty separately for each of the three named views. This baseline adds no neural or statistical model fitting.

## Operational burdens and age shares

For the three direct model views and the corresponding component-derived DALY and prevalence-based YLD views, report operational counts/burden-years, broad-age totals and 65+/80+ shares within 45+. Use the two already specified population procedures: last-eight-year implied-population log trend and persistence. Use the preserved prevalence-implied population forecasts and historical population-error blocks as a common denominator across outcomes, after verifying agreement of native Number/Rate denominators across outcomes within rounding. This common denominator makes component additivity exact; do not mix different population assumptions in a sum.

Pair population and rate errors by historical residual origin, retain all ages/sexes/horizons jointly and transform whole blocks before quantiles. Source lower/upper bounds and seed spread are not predictive draws. YLD/YLL/DALY totals have units of modeled burden-years; death totals have units of modeled events. Verify against each outcome's native source Number, with only floating-point tolerance for the component identity. Report original-scale MAE, share percentage-point error, interval coverage/width and 50/80 WIS; do not pool these units. These analyses concern modeled burden and cannot forecast individual survival or establish service-capacity requirements.

## Earlier mortality history: separate fixed sensitivity

The source contains 1980–1989 for deaths/YLLs. Retain these years in a separately labeled 1980-start versus 1990-start comparison at origin 2018 for all six GCC targets. Expand both donor and target histories in the earlier-start arm; this is an available-history sensitivity, not isolated target-history learning efficiency. Hold the compact TCN at the locked cold-start settings (16 channels, weight decay 0.001, 50 epochs), the five seeds and adaptation penalty 1 in both arms; include adapted/unadapted TCN and the same damped-ETS procedure. No grid search or family selection is performed. Both neural history arms use the same GPU device and training code, so history comparisons do not mix CPU/GPU ensembles. Compare point errors at each horizon/sex/age, including 80+; do not claim predictive-interval validation from this endpoint sensitivity. Older modeled mortality/YLL history need not represent additional independent observations. This sensitivity is distinct from the later 15/20/29-year target-history learning curves and from tuned CPU core models.

## Execution and preservation

Use `agpu` with one numerical thread per worker. The direct core runs on CPU, preserving device comparability with the completed primary/secondary experiments; sixteen CPU fit workers and bounded parallel case-assembly workers are planned. The independent earlier-history sensitivity can use four GPU workers concurrently. Prior real-data benchmarking found CPU faster for the main compact-network queue; extra GPU work must remain scientifically specified, not added merely to occupy hardware.

Require synthetic tests and source/specification hashes before production. Save each fit with its actual outcome, target, origin, settings, seed, device and source identity. Verify cached jobs on resume; reject changed code/configuration/inputs or mixed devices. Commit all 24 core issued ledgers before any new core final scoring. Each derived/sensitivity analysis commits its points and distributions before its scoring; its specification is fixed before the core fitting begins. No earlier forecast model, locked data/design file, calibration run or `AGENTS.md` is edited.

Independent checks must reconstruct actual-outcome source matches, selection cutoffs, residual banks, joint quantiles/sums, score arithmetic and representative saved neural/non-neural checkpoints without refitting them. Additional tests cover early-death history exclusion from the common core, future-row perturbation invariance, exact ratio-history boundaries, component pairing, common-denominator count identities and input/output integrity. All fallback cells remain explicit. Reports retain unfavorable results and distinguish supporting evidence from the primary conclusion.
